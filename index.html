Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OVERview ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. By using OVERview, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

OVERview is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide the service:

  • Financial data you enter manually — account balances, transactions, goals, debts, and categories. This data is stored locally on your device and/or in your personal iCloud account.
  • Usage data — anonymous analytics about how features are used, to help us improve the app. This data does not identify you personally.
  • Device information — device type and OS version, used only for technical support purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the core functionality of the app
  • To sync your data across your personal devices via iCloud (if enabled)
  • To respond to support requests
  • To analyze anonymous usage patterns and fix bugs

3. Data Storage & Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is stored in your personal Apple iCloud account and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your iCloud data.

We implement industry-standard security practices to protect any data we do process. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

OVERview may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Apple iCloud — for data synchronization (optional)
  • OpenAI / Anthropic — for AI Assistant features. Queries sent to the AI assistant may be processed by third-party AI providers. Please do not include sensitive personal data in AI queries.

5. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal financial data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Ever.

6. Children's Privacy

OVERview is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data at any time within the app
  • Disable iCloud sync at any time in your device settings
  • Request deletion of any data we hold by contacting us

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.
